Pastor Mrs Abiodun Bukola Olutide

Zonal Mummy

Pastor Mrs Abiodun Bukola Olutide is a devoted minister of God, educationist, counsellor, and mentor with 26 years of ministry experience. She serves at RCCG Halleluyah Sanctuary as WPICZ and is passionate about teaching the Word of God, prayer, counselling, and spiritual growth.

She holds a B.Sc. in Education (Accounting) and a Master’s degree in Educational Management.

She also serves as the Director of School at God’s Blessing Comprehensive College, where she promotes academic excellence and godly character. Her passion is to see lives transformed and people fulfil their God-given purpose.

Pastor Ajibola Salawu

Zonal Youth Pastor

Pastor Salawu Ajibola is an Assistant Provincial Youth Pastor of RCCG Oyo 18, Zonal Youth Pastor of Halleluyah Sanctuary, and a Parish Pastor, with over 15 years of experience in youth ministry. He holds a B.MLS in Medical Microbiology and Parasitology.

He has served in various Christian leadership positions, including President of RCF Oyo State Chapter, President of RCF UCH, Vice President of the Association of Christian Fellowships UCH, and Evangelism Secretary of YAYA Oyo 18.

He is passionate about youth development, teaching, and preaching, and currently serves as the Oyo State Chairman of Young Medical Laboratory Scientists.

Deacon Opeyemi Adejuwon

Provincial Youth Pastor · Oyo 18

Deacon Opeyemi Adejuwon is the Provincial Youth Pastor of RCCG Oyo 18, with over 20 years of experience in youth ministry.

He holds a B. Agric in Economics and Extension, an M.Sc. in Extension and Rural Development, an M.Sc. in Project Management, and a Ph.D. in Rural Sociology.

He is passionate about youth development and empowering young people to fulfil their God-given purpose. His awards include the NCCF Award of Excellence as Zonal Coordinator, Zaria Zone, the 2025 Man of the Year Award by Peculiar Zone YAYA, and the 2025 Award of Excellence by Oyo 18 YAYA.

Deaconess Olaloye Happiness

Song Minister

Deaconess Happiness Olaloye is a devoted child of God, a passionate worshipper, and a committed worker in God's vineyard. She serves faithfully in the choir department of RCCG Peculiar People's Parish, Oyo 18, where she uses her gift of singing to glorify God and inspire others through worship.

Deacon John Andrew

Song Minister

Deacon John Ogbor Andrew is a passionate music minister from Benue State with over a decade of experience in music ministry.

He currently serves as the YAYA Music Director 1 in RCCG Oyo 18, where he is committed to using music to glorify God, inspire worship, and impact lives. He holds a B.Sc. degree from Ajayi Crowther University and has a strong passion for music and prophetic ministry.

Through his years of service, he has witnessed God's supernatural move and the winning of souls through ministry. He has received an Award of Excellence as a Region 42 Executive and served as Vice President of the Fellowship at The Polytechnic, Ibadan.
